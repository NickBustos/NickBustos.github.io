About me

Hi, I'm Nick Bustos, a full stack developer from Argentina and currently living in Spain and working on several projects.

I started programming at 16 through a school program, writing my first lines of code in Visual Basic. While still studying, I took on freelance work building and maintaining sites on Wix and Shopify.

I later moved to Spain, where I earned a Higher Vocational Degree in Web Application Development (DAW), a two-year professional program, while working part-time managing WordPress sites and handling DevOps tasks, from server maintenance to deployment environments.

A full-time job offer led me to put my Bachelor's in Computer Engineering on hold, which I plan to resume, and to work as a full-stack developer on projects built with Angular, React, Python and Java, including hybrid mobile apps with Ionic.

Currently, as a hobby, I'm building video games with Godot (GDScript), and I really enjoy the mix of creativity and planning that goes into them.
